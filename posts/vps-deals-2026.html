A working note on how I run the editorial pipeline behind progeofde.tech.

The problem with $10/year VPS lists

Most "best cheap VPS" articles recycle the same three providers, paste a coupon code, and never mention what happens at month 5. ProGeoFDE takes a different angle: I document the price you actually pay at renewal, the network restrictions, and whether the listed RAM is shared or dedicated.

What I check before adding a provider

  • Published price locked for at least 12 months, not just first term
  • Acceptable abuse policy (no "your server is suspended pending review" with no recourse)
  • Real IPv4 or a documented IPv6-only path
  • Publicly available TOS that doesn't auto-renew at 5x the listed price
